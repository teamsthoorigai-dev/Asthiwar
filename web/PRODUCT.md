# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Prospective homeowners and property owners in and around Coimbatore who are evaluating an architecture and construction partner. They need to understand how ASTHIWAR combines design, engineering, execution, cost awareness, and sustainable building choices before beginning a project.

## Product Purpose

Present ASTHIWAR as a coordinated design-and-build practice, make its architectural point of view tangible through completed work and material thinking, and give prospective clients clear paths to explore services, estimate costs, and start a project conversation.

## Brand Personality

Thoughtful, grounded, and precise. The experience should feel materially aware and quietly confident, with the calm of climate-responsive architecture and the clarity of a well-resolved construction detail.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic real-estate luxury, glossy developer marketing, templated sustainability icon grids, decorative greenwashing, and interfaces that feel disconnected from construction materials or place. Preserve the image-led, architectural composition established by the site and the supplied references.

## Design Principles

- Show material and environmental decisions through real architectural imagery and specific language.
- Make architecture, engineering, and execution read as one continuous process.
- Let hierarchy and proportion carry the design; keep decoration subordinate to the work.
- Pair aspirational storytelling with clear, practical routes to services, costs, and contact.
- Keep claims grounded and label unverified project information honestly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast and keyboard access. Maintain visible focus states, semantic headings and lists, useful image alternatives, reduced-motion accommodations, and layouts that remain readable from 320px mobile screens through large desktop displays.
